03 · Priorities

Nine fixes, in priority order.

01 – 09

  1. 01Fix tracking first
  2. 02Respond faster
  3. 03One page per need
  4. 04Win local search
  5. 05Search first
  6. 06Educate and build trust
  7. 07Respect privacy
  8. 08Speak their language
  9. 09Report in one view

  1. Fix
    Track form fills, WhatsApp taps and calls as separate conversions in GA4 and Tag Manager.
    Why
    The real result is a counseling call or an appointment, not a click.

  2. Fix
    Counselor replies within minutes in working hours, an automatic WhatsApp acknowledgement after hours, and every lead tagged qualified, booked or visited.
    Why
    Speed lifts conversion, and tagged outcomes teach the ad platform who actually books.

  3. Fix
    Focused landing pages by treatment area and branch, each with one call to action, doctor credentials and fast mobile load.
    Why
    Matching ad to page raises conversion and Quality Score, which lowers cost per click.

  4. Fix
    Accurate hours, phone and services per branch, prompt review replies, fresh photos and regular posts on Google Business Profile.
    Why
    Many patients search near me. A strong profile earns visibility before any ad spend.

  5. Fix
    Put the main budget on high-intent search plus brand protection, with a strong negative keyword list.
    Why
    People searching already have a need, so less budget goes to casual interest.

  6. Fix
    Doctor-led explainers and counselor Q&A in Telugu, Hindi and English, with no outcome promises.
    Why
    Choosing cancer care takes time. Education builds trust before the first call.

  7. Fix
    No condition-based audiences or remarketing. Use consented first-party lists, content engagement and contextual placements, checked against current platform policy.
    Why
    It keeps the account safe and protects patients.

  8. Fix
    Test Telugu and Hindi ads and landing pages for Hyderabad and wider Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.
    Why
    Families decide in their own language, and regional keywords are usually less crowded.

  9. Fix
    An automated weekly dashboard with spend, leads, qualified leads, appointments and cost per qualified lead by campaign and branch, with alerts for spend spikes or tracking breaks.
    Why
    One reliable view replaces manual reports. This is where my engineering background helps most.

04 · Campaign structure

One budget. One patient journey.

  • 55% Search: people already looking for care
  • 20% YouTube and Meta: education that builds trust
  • 15% Local and Maps: near-me searches
  • 10% Testing: new audiences, creatives and pages

A starting hypothesis, to be tested and adjusted with real data.

The patient journey

  1. AwareA family searches or watches an educational video.
  2. ConsiderThey read treatment pages, reviews and doctor profiles.
  3. ContactA form, a WhatsApp message or a call.
  4. QualifyA counselor responds quickly and tags the lead.
  5. VisitThe appointment is booked and attended at the right branch.
  6. LearnOutcomes flow back into the ads, so budget follows what works.

06 · First 90 days

Three phases. Clear deliverables.

Phase oneDays 1–30

Learn and fix

  • Audit ad accounts, tracking, landing pages and Google Business Profile, and record baseline numbers.
  • Meet the counselor, medical and legal teams to learn the approval process.
  • Fix conversion tracking and start a lead-status feedback sheet.
  • Write the compliance checklist and approved-phrases library.

Phase twoDays 31–60

Build and test

  • Restructure search campaigns and negative keywords.
  • Launch landing pages for the top treatment areas and branches.
  • Start a test calendar with a documented test log.
  • Launch Telugu and Hindi variants.
  • Go live with the weekly dashboard.

Phase threeDays 61–90

Scale what works

  • Shift budget to the sources that bring qualified leads and visits.
  • Scale winners in careful steps.
  • Add doctor-led YouTube and Meta education.
  • Present a 90-day review and a next-quarter plan.
